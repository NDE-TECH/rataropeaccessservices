Integrated access and inspection

Technical inspection delivered directly at the workface

Conventional access systems can be slow or impractical around flare structures, tanks, pipe racks, offshore modules, chimneys and tall structures.

Rope access allows an appropriately configured team to reach the required inspection points while maintaining close contact with the component. IAIS India and IAIS UAE plan the rope system and NDT technique together, accounting for surface preparation, equipment positioning, calibration, measurement coverage and data recording.

The inspection technique, acceptance criteria and reporting format are confirmed against the client procedure, applicable code and asset condition before execution.

Typical client information required

  • Asset type, material and dimensions
  • Inspection locations and quantities
  • Drawings, isometrics or photographs
  • Required NDT method and coverage
  • Applicable code, procedure or acceptance criteria
  • Operating temperature and process condition
  • Access limitations and site restrictions
  • Reporting and data-format expectations
Inspection methods

Rope access NDT capabilities

Method availability is subject to technical feasibility, surface condition, geometry and the client-approved procedure.

01 / UTM

Ultrasonic Thickness Measurement

Remaining-wall measurement and corrosion monitoring at selected grid or spot locations.

  • Piping, tanks, vessels and structures
  • Elevation-referenced data recording
  • Local thinning identification
02 / UT

Conventional Ultrasonic Testing

Pulse-echo inspection for thickness, lamination checks and weld examination where technically applicable.

  • Weld and parent-material inspection
  • Angle-beam and straight-beam techniques
  • Procedure-based calibration
03 / ADVANCED UT

PAUT & TOFD

Encoded or manual advanced ultrasonic inspection for welds and selected components.

  • Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing
  • Time of Flight Diffraction
  • Scan data and image-based reporting
04 / SURFACE NDT

Magnetic Particle Testing

Detection of surface and near-surface discontinuities in suitable ferromagnetic materials.

  • Welds, attachments and connections
  • Visible or fluorescent technique as specified
  • Local surface preparation support
05 / SURFACE NDT

Liquid Penetrant Testing

Surface-breaking discontinuity detection on suitable non-porous materials.

  • Welds and machined areas
  • Visible or fluorescent technique as specified
  • Documented indication assessment
06 / VISUAL

Close Visual Inspection

Direct visual assessment supported by measurement tools and photographic documentation.

  • Corrosion, cracking and deformation
  • Weld, coating and connection condition
  • High-resolution location records
07 / MATERIAL ID

Positive Material Identification

Alloy verification using portable equipment appropriate to the material and requirement.

  • Component and piping verification
  • Location-based result capture
  • Material-mix-up investigation support
08 / HARDNESS

Portable Hardness Testing

Field hardness measurement for suitable materials, surfaces and component geometries.

  • Weld, HAZ and parent material
  • Method selected for the application
  • Traceable test-location reporting
09 / MAPPING

Corrosion Mapping & Monitoring

Structured thickness collection to evaluate the distribution and progression of wall loss.

  • Defined grids and condition-based expansion
  • Repeat-location monitoring
  • Tabulated and visualised results
