Service overview

Wind Turbine Services delivered through planned rope access

Task planning considers turbine status, weather limits, rescue arrangements, blade positioning, dropped-object controls and the specific work instruction.

IAIS India and IAIS UAE deploy IRATA-certified rope access personnel and appropriately qualified trade or inspection technicians. Team composition, equipment, method statement, risk assessment and rescue arrangements are finalized for the actual site conditions and client requirements.

Information needed for planning

  • Turbine make, model and location
  • Scope by turbine and component
  • OEM procedure and acceptance criteria
  • Weather and shutdown window
  • Repair materials and technical instruction
  • Reporting and image requirements
