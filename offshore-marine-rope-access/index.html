Marine operating environment

Access planned around offshore controls and asset availability

Offshore and marine projects combine access difficulty with restricted logistics, weather exposure, simultaneous operations and limited opportunities for re-mobilisation.

IAIS India develops the rope-access system, technical work scope and mobilisation package together. Team composition can combine rope access personnel with NDT technicians or maintenance trades according to the approved project requirement.

Personnel documents, medicals, offshore training, visas, travel readiness and equipment certification are confirmed against the client’s mobilisation matrix before deployment.

Information required for planning

  • Asset type and project location
  • Work areas, elevations and access drawings
  • Inspection or maintenance quantities
  • Applicable procedure and acceptance standard
  • Offshore rotation and duty hours
  • Required training, medicals and visa status
  • Accommodation, transfers and baggage limits
  • Reporting and client-document requirements
Service capability

Offshore and marine rope access services

Each scope remains subject to technical feasibility, safe access, client procedures and project-specific personnel requirements.

01 / NDT

Rope Access NDT

Close-contact inspection on elevated and restricted structures.

  • UTM and conventional UT
  • PAUT and TOFD where applicable
  • MT, PT and visual testing
  • PMI and portable hardness testing
02 / STRUCTURAL

Structural Inspection

Condition surveys across offshore topside and marine structures.

  • Members, welds and connections
  • Modules, derricks and masts
  • Flare booms and elevated systems
  • Corrosion and deformation surveys
03 / THICKNESS

UTM & Corrosion Surveys

Location-controlled thickness collection for integrity review.

  • Piping and process systems
  • Tanks, vessels and structural steel
  • Grid and spot measurements
  • Repeat-location monitoring
04 / MAINTENANCE

Mechanical Support

Preventive and corrective work at elevated locations.

  • Fastener and component replacement
  • Support and bracket maintenance
  • Minor dismantling and installation
  • Rigging and tool-hauling support
05 / CORROSION CONTROL

Surface & Coating Work

Local protection of exposed offshore and marine steelwork.

  • Manual and power-tool preparation
  • Rust and coating removal
  • Primer and protective coating
  • Touch-up and stripe coating
06 / MARINE ASSETS

Vessel & Terminal Support

Inspection and maintenance support for marine structures.

  • Ship external structures
  • Cargo-area access support
  • Jetty and terminal structures
  • High-level visual documentation
