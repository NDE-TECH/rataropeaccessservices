Why rope access?

A controlled access method for complex industrial work

Rope access provides a practical way to position trained personnel at elevated, restricted or geometrically difficult work locations.

When correctly planned and supervised, it can reduce scaffolding requirements, shorten mobilisation time and limit interference with plant operations. The method is especially valuable where inspection points are distributed across tanks, flare systems, structures, vessels, offshore assets, façades and wind turbines.

IAIS India selects personnel according to both rope access competence and the technical discipline required for the work.

Faster mobilisation Reduced access-system installation time.
